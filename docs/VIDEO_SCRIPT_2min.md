# 🎬 ReStock AI — 2–3 Minute Submission Video Script

**Rules to honor:** show the **live Alternate Clouds deployment** (not localhost),
show **where the human steps in**, state the problem then show the solution, skip
long intros. Record your screen with the live URL in the address bar visible.

> Live URL to keep visible: `alternate-restless-forest-3113-github-app.alternatefutures.ai`

---

### [0:00–0:20] Problem (talk over the live dashboard)
> "Distributors make thousands of restock decisions a week. Automate all of them and one bad order burns the budget; do them by hand and the team drowns. This is **ReStock AI** — running live on Alternate Clouds right now." *(point to the URL in the address bar)*

### [0:20–0:50] The agent runs — show the three routes
- Click **Run batch**.
> "A pre-trained agent just cleared the queue: it **auto-approved** the routine orders, **blocked** the ones over our budget ceiling, and **escalated** the risky ones for a human."
- Hover a **BLOCKED** row, then an **ESCALATED** row's rationale.
> "The AI handles the volume. For the risky calls, an LLM explains *why* — but it never makes the decision."

### [0:50–1:40] Where the human steps in (the key beat)
- Point to the **Human approval queue**.
> "Here's the human-in-the-loop. These expensive or uncertain orders are waiting for a person."
- Click **Approve** on one.
> "I approve it — the order executes, stock replenishes, and it issues a real **purchase order number**." *(point to the PO tag)*
- Click **Reject** on another; point to the **Adaptive gate** readout rising.
> "If I reject one, the system raises its confidence bar and escalates more cautiously next time — it learns from the human, without ever retraining the model."

*(If you configured Discord: show the escalation arriving in Discord and tap Approve on your phone — strongest possible version of this beat.)*

### [1:40–2:10] Reacting to the real world
- Click **📡 Ingest demand**.
> "And it's not just manual — a demand signal arrives and the agent re-evaluates the affected products on its own. New demand in, decisions out."

### [2:10–2:40] Close
> "So: the AI owns the routine volume, the human owns the judgment, it does real work — purchase orders, approvals, a reactive demand loop — all deployed live on Alternate Clouds. That's ReStock AI. Thanks for watching."

## 🎥 Recording tips
- **Keep the browser address bar visible** the whole time — it proves the live deployment.
- Do a dry run once so **Run batch** and the LLM are warm.
- Toggle to the theme that looks best on your screen (dark usually pops more).
- Record at 1080p; keep it **under 3 minutes**.
- Upload as **unlisted YouTube** or **Google Drive → Share → Anyone with the link**, then paste the link into the PDF.
- Watch it back once in an incognito window to confirm the link plays.
