The problem

Distributors make thousands of restock decisions every week. Automate all of them and a single mispriced or oversized order burns the budget. Do them by hand and the team drowns in routine approvals. Neither extreme is acceptable — the routine volume needs automation, but the risky calls need human judgment.

The solution

ReStock AI is an AI-driven replenishment officer. A pre-trained Q-Learning agent decides how much to reorder for each product, and a dual safety gate routes every decision one of three ways:

  • ✅ Auto-execute — routine, confident, affordable orders run without a human.
  • 🛎️ Escalate to a human — expensive, low-confidence or unfamiliar orders are queued for one-click approval, with a plain-English rationale written by an LLM.
  • ⛔ Block — orders over the quantity cap, cost ceiling or remaining budget are refused outright.
Where the human steps in: escalated orders wait in the approval queue (and are pushed to Discord with Approve/Reject links). A human approves or rejects; approval issues a real purchase order, rejection voids it and makes the gate more cautious. The LLM only explains — it never decides.

An honest note on the model

The Q-table in this build is a synthesised, explainable policy covering all 81 states — not a trained one — and it is a drop-in artifact: a notebook-exported Q-table with the same schema plugs in unchanged. The feedback loop adapts the gate, never the learned values, and the LLM only writes rationales. We chose explainability and a reliable live demo, and we are transparent about it.
